## 📊 Featured Projects

### 🚢 Titanic Dataset Exploration
A full exploratory analysis on passenger survival data.
- Compared survival rates across gender, age, and class.
- Identified trends such as:
  - Higher female survival rate.
  - 3rd-class male passengers as the largest group of casualties.
  - Majority of passengers boarded from Southampton.
- Used visualizations like:
  - `sns.countplot`, `sns.histplot`, `sns.heatmap`, `sns.pairplot`, and `FacetGrid`.

### 🍽️ Tips Dataset Exploration
Studied restaurant tipping behavior based on:
- Total bill, gender, and day of the week.
- Visualized data distributions and correlations between numerical and categorical variables.
